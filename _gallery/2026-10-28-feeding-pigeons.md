While I was out on a drizzly morning walk in the People's Square (人民广场),
this passerby squatted down to feed some pigeons. I quickly snapped a few photos
of the action before I realized my shutter speed was too slow. Happily, I find
the accidental blurriness imparts a dynamic, almost angelic effect to the
flurry.
